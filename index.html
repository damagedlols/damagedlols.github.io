HEY WHAT'S UP GUYS!!! My name is Damaged (well, that's my gaming name anyway) and I'm a 13-year-old gamer from the USA! I'm in 8th grade and I love video games SO MUCH. My mom says I play too much but I think she just doesn't understand how important gaming is 😂

My favorite games are Fortnite (I'm pretty good, I have like 50+ wins), Minecraft (I build epic castles), and any anime games (Demon Slayer is my favorite anime EVER!!!).

Okay so here's the important part: I'm also a Christian! I go to church every Sunday with my family and I'm in the youth group. I try to be a good example and not rage too much when I lose (but sometimes it's hard!!!).

I started my YouTube and TikTok channels last year because I wanted to share my gameplay with other people. It's been really fun and I've met some cool friends online. My dream is to become a big streamer one day but my mom says I have to finish school first 😅

Fun facts about me: I have a dog named Max, my favorite food is pizza, I play soccer at school, and I'm learning to play guitar (but I'm not very good yet).
