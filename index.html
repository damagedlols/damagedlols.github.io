ABOUT ME

Get to know the gamer behind the controller!

Hey, I'm Damaged - a 13-year-old Xbox gamer who's passionate about gaming, creating content, and learning to code!

I've been gaming since second grade when my dad got me my first Xbox! I was so excited I literally screamed when I opened it on Christmas morning. I've been hooked ever since!

My favorite games are Fortnite, Minecraft, Red Dead Redemption 2, and Call of Duty Black Ops 7. I love the thrill of battle royale, the creativity of building, and the stories in adventure games. I'm pretty decent at Fortnite - I've got more wins than I can count!

When I'm not gaming, I'm creating content for my YouTube channels, TikTok, and Instagram. I post gaming highlights, funny moments, tutorials, and montages. I'm also learning how to code - I started with HTML and CSS (this website is my project!), and I want to learn JavaScript and maybe even make my own game someday!

I have an awesome dog named Lulu who sits with me while I stream sometimes. She's a golden retriever and she's the best gaming companion ever!

My goal is to build an awesome community of gamers who love playing together and creating cool content. Whether you're here for the gameplay, the laughs, or just to hang out, you're welcome here!
